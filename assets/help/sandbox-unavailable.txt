Tinman needs Bubblewrap {{required}} or newer. {{found}}

Tinman never executes a program outside a sandbox, so this is a hard
requirement rather than an optional feature, and no flag lifts it.

Install instructions, and why the version floor exists:
https://github.com/dmytri/tinman#requirements

Everything that executes nothing still works: tinman --help, tinman man,
and tinman completions.
